Introduction

The modest fashion market is experiencing strong growth.

  • Female clientele seeking elegance and modesty.
  • Need to modernize offerings through professional digital channels.

The goal of the byines project is to design a high-end and efficient virtual showroom.

Problematic

Despite the success of their 3 physical stores and a rising Instagram page with close to 100,000 followers, the brand faces the limitations of traditional sales and social media:

  • Geographical limits: Exclusive dependence on 3 local stores.
  • Sales by message (WhatsApp/Instagram): Disorganized, risk of errors, massive waste of time.
  • Inventory management: No centralized, real-time tracking (overselling).
  • Brand image: Unprofessional customer experience online.

A centralized, automated, and elegant solution has become a necessity.

Technologies Used

Frontend (Client)

  • HTML5: Semantic structure
  • CSS3: Variables, Flexbox, Responsive Design
  • JavaScript (ES6): DOM, AJAX, Interactive Cart

Backend (Server)

  • PHP 8.x (OOP): Business logic and controllers
  • MySQL (PDO): Prepared statements and security
  • MVC Architecture: Separation of Views / Models
